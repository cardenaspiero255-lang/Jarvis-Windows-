# JARVIS Windows · Synthetic «Hey Jarvis» stress test

**Purpose:** continue measuring and improving wake-word detection without collecting voices from other people. This diagnostic NEVER replaces genuine human positive samples or a commercial rights review.

## Test matrix

| Job | Source WAVs | Augmentations | Evaluations | Inference |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| `synthetic-smoke-real-verifier` | 3 positive + 3 negative | clean, deterministic white noise 20 dB / 10 dB SNR, simulated attenuated echo | 24 | Real built-in openWakeWord + Whisper secondary verifier (only after acoustic trigger) |
| `synthetic-campaign-240` | 30 positive + 30 negative | same 4 | 240 | Real built-in openWakeWord only |

Generated by **actual Windows System.Speech SAPI installed voices**, not human speakers. The `synthetic_source_voice_count` field reports unique voice IDs installed on that runner. Multiple phrases, speaking rates and transformations are NOT multiple independent people. Accent labels are not guessed; SAPI locale is recorded solely as runtime metadata.

Sample positives begin with `Hey Jarvis,` followed by natural commands. Hard negatives include the isolated name in a sentence, `Hey Travis`, unrelated speech, and silence-adjacent situations. Genuine human negatives are already handled in the independent public corpus workflows and remain separate.

The production threshold **0.4** is checked against `wake_word.openwakeword._THRESHOLD`. A mismatch causes an explicit failure. No thresholds, phrases, waveforms or verification rules may be edited to favor a score. The synthetic samples serve **development diagnostics**, not an untouched external holdout.

## Interpretation

JSON proof `status=SYNTHETIC_ONLY_MEASURED` means real inference ran successfully, **not** that wake detection is accurate. Read the measured rates and raw decisions to evaluate trade-offs:

- `synthetic_positive_baseline_recall`: fraction of synthetic positive conditions that triggered first-stage wake.
- `synthetic_positive_verified_recall`: fraction of synthetic positive conditions accepted by BOTH acoustic wake and local Whisper; available only in the smoke job.
- `synthetic_negative_baseline_false_activations`: synthetic negative cases triggering first-stage wake.
- `synthetic_negative_verified_false_activations`: negatives accepted by BOTH stages, available only in the smoke job.

All human FNR/recall values and real-world false activations/hour remain **unknown**. Noise addition and attenuation simulate a condition; they do not prove physical microphone, acoustics, speaker identity or actual recording distance. A failure in a downstream workflow must not be disguised as a synthetic pass.

## Reproduce in CI

Workflow: [Windows Synthetic Hey Jarvis Stress (Real Models)](../.github/workflows/synthetic-wake-stress-windows.yml).

PR modifications to the scorer, generator, tests or workflow run the smoke and the 240-condition campaign. All source WAVs are created only in the disposable Windows runner and are deleted before job completion. The workflow uploads *only* JSON metrics, not speech or model assets.

Two separate local components:
- `scripts/windows-synthetic-wake-stress.ps1` selects installed voices, emits WAVs and records original hashes and labels.
- `jarvis-studio-backend/synthetic_wake_stress.py` checks metadata and WAV SHA-256, deterministically augments audio and runs **real ONNX inference**. Whisper is invoked only when `-WithWhisper` is selected, and only on model triggers. This is the same two-stage policy as the existing runtime.

Do **not** commit or ship the downloaded openWakeWord pretrained models through this workflow. The model's non-commercial license is still a separate publication blocker. Installed SAPI voices are exercised on Windows runners, not distributed as standalone weights. Piper voice model licenses depend on their individual model cards and training data, so they are not presumed commercial either.

## Next controlled experiment

Analyze per-variant misses and false activations from the receipts, compare the development trend to the independently sourced human negatives, and only then propose a specific code/model change. Add a failing regression for a reproduced error before fixing it. Do not tune to an existing holdout and then claim a new blind evaluation. Genuine adult volunteers' recordings or a separately licensed representative human dataset will still be necessary for human-positive release certification.

## Evaluación adversarial detectada y comparación A/B

La campaña inicial ejecutó 240 condiciones y 24 condiciones con Whisper. En
la prueba de 24, el verificador con prompt `Jarvis` rechazó cinco de doce
positivos sintéticos y aceptó uno de doce negativos, incluyendo el adversarial
«Hey Travis». No es un resultado de voces humanas ni un indicador de producción.

Para comprobar si el prompt inicial sesga a Whisper, el smoke ejecuta ahora
**ambas configuraciones** sobre exactamente el mismo PCM y el mismo disparo
acústico: `_do_transcribe(audio, "Jarvis")` (producción) y
`_do_transcribe(audio, "")` (candidato experimental). Se reportan por separado
el recall sintético y las falsas activaciones de ambas, sin cambiar la
configuración de producción ni decidir con los mismos casos que una opción
supera una evaluación independiente. Un cambio de producto futuro requerirá
nuevos controles humanos y pruebas adversariales congeladas.
